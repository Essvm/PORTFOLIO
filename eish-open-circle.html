EISH — OPEN CIRCLE

Community / Brand Identity / Character System / Campaign

BRANDING / COMMUNITY
Real life. More fun. EISH is a self-initiated identity concept for a real-life experiences community, bringing people together through hosted activities, local discovery and new friendships. Inspired by Cairo, the system connects an open-circle motif with expressive characters and warm, tactile brand applications.
THE BRIEF & GOAL
Create a welcoming identity for a community that helps people meet, explore their city and spend more time together in real life.
CREATIVE DIRECTION
Warm cream, sunny yellow and ink black; open circles, a playful explorer character, natural Cairo settings and tactile embroidered details.
MY ROLE
Brand concept, creative direction, identity and character exploration, branded touchpoints, and AI-assisted campaign visualization.
✦
Workflow: Self-initiated brand concept. Creative direction and AI-assisted identity, merchandise and campaign visualizations. Includes an early art direction study alongside the Open Circle identity.
