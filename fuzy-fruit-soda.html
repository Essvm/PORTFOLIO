FUZY — FRUIT OUT OF LINE

Fruit Soda / Brand Identity / Packaging / Campaign

BRANDING / PACKAGING
Fruit Out of Line: a fictional Egyptian fruit soda concept built around unexpected flavor pairings. A cohesive family of Mango + Lime, Guava + Mint, and Berry + Lemon spans packaging, editorial campaigns, and refreshing product imagery.
THE BRIEF & GOAL
Build a bold, contemporary fruit soda identity for young adults, with a consistent three-flavor packaging family.
CREATIVE DIRECTION
Cream foundations, bold black typography, overlapping curved forms, and distinctive flavor color pairings.
MY ROLE
Creative direction, AI-assisted visual production, graphic design, and image refinement.
✦
Workflow: Self-initiated fictional brand concept. Creative direction and AI-assisted packaging and campaign visualizations.
